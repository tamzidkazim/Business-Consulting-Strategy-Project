# Industry Group Project – Future Foundations Consulting

## Overview

This industry consulting project was completed for Future Foundations Consulting (FFC) as part of a university industry engagement program.

The project involved analysing the organisation's business environment and developing strategic recommendations to support future growth, market positioning, customer engagement, and long-term sustainability.

The report evaluated internal and external business factors, identified opportunities for expansion, and proposed practical strategies to improve brand awareness, client acquisition, and organisational performance.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyse the current business environment of Future Foundations Consulting
- Identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats
- Assess competitors and market positioning
- Conduct market research and target market analysis
- Develop strategic business recommendations
- Create measurable marketing goals and KPIs
- Design an implementation-focused action plan
